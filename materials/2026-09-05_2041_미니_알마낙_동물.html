Penguin

Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly. They can swim very well. They live in cold places.

🐒
Monkey

Monkeys live in jungles. They can climb trees very well. They eat fruit and nuts.

🐘
Elephant

Elephants are the largest land animals. They have long trunks. They are very smart.

🐰
Rabbit

Rabbits have long ears and soft fur. They can run very fast. They eat grass and vegetables.

  • A penguin can hold its breath under water for 5 minutes!
  • A monkey can jump 4 to 5 meters!
  • An elephant can hear with its feet!
  • A rabbit can move its ears in different directions!
